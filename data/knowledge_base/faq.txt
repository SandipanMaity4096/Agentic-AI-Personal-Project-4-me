Frequently Asked Questions

1) Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of purchase if the product has not been fully consumed.
Refund requests are reviewed in 2-3 business days.

2) Password Reset
Users can reset passwords from the login page by clicking 'Forgot Password'.
Password reset links expire in 30 minutes.

3) Delivery Delay
Standard delivery can take 3-7 business days.
If delivery exceeds 10 business days, users can raise a support ticket.

4) Billing Errors
Duplicate charge complaints should include transaction ID and billing date.
Finance support resolves billing disputes within 5 business days.

5) Service Outage
Known service outages are posted on the status page.
Critical enterprise outages are prioritized for immediate incident response.
